Kingdom Giving Categories

Whether honoring God with your tithe or sowing a special thanksgiving seed, choose your preferred category:

Tithes & Firstfruits

Honor the Lord with the first 10% of your income and firstfruits as a testimony of His covenant faithfulness.

"Bring the whole tithe into the storehouse..." — Malachi 3:10

Weekly Offering

Freewill weekly offerings given during Sunday Celebration Services and midweek Bible studies.

"Give, and it will be given to you..." — Luke 6:38

Building & Expansion Fund

Seeds sown toward sanctuary expansion, infrastructure development, and physical facility upgrades.

"Go up into the mountains and bring down timber and build my house..." — Haggai 1:8

Missions & Community Outreach

Funding evangelism outreaches, welfare packages for widows/indigent members, and rural church planting.

"He who is kind to the poor lends to the Lord..." — Proverbs 19:17

Special Seed & Thanksgiving

Personal thanksgiving seeds for God's protection, birthday celebrations, child dedications, or prophetic vows.

"Offer to God a sacrifice of thanksgiving..." — Psalm 50:14
